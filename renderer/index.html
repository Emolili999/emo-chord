🎹 Emochord Studio – Practice & Controls Guide

1. Practice Modes

  • 🎯 Practice (Wait Mode): Notes flow down toward the keys. The song pauses at each note until you hit the right key, with visual guides. Accompaniment notes remain synced!
  • ⏱️ Rhythm Practice: The song flows continuously; timing accuracy awards points and combo multipliers.
  • 🤚 Right Hand / ✋ Left Hand: Pick one hand to practice; the other hand is played automatically by the studio!

2. Computer Keyboard Controls (2 Octaves)

  • Upper Row White Keys (C4–G5): Q W E R T Y U I O P Ğ Ü
  • Upper Row Black Keys: 2 3   5 6 7   9 0 =
  • Lower Row White Keys (C3–E4): Z X C V B N M Ö Ç .
  • Lower Row Black Keys: S D   G H J   L Ş

3. A–B Loop Practice & Timeline

Click anywhere on the glowing top timeline bar to scrub immediately. Set A and B points to loop difficult sections on repeat.

4. Quick Shortcuts

  • Settings Drawer: M key
  • Sustain Pedal: Spacebar
  • Octave / Transpose: ← → and ↓ ↑ arrow keys
  • Mute All Notes: Esc
